TOF sensor (Waveshare TOF Laser Range Sensor D, UART mode):
Reconfigure sensor baud to 115200 via TOF Assistant BEFORE wiring in —
SoftwareSerial can't reliably hold the 921600 default.

Sensor TX  ─────────────────────────► Arduino D7 (RX)
Sensor RX  ─────────────────────────► Arduino D8 (TX, unused for reading)
Sensor VCC ─────────────────────────► per datasheet
Sensor GND ─────────────────────────► GND
